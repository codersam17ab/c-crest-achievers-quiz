⚙️ Terms & Conditions
  • Time Limited: 20 seconds. Time-out automatically passes the question to the other team.
  • Wrong Answer: Question passes to the next team. A correct answer does not shift the turn immediately.
  • Right Answer: Correct option becomes green and the active team gets +1.
  • Score System: +1 for each correct answer; no point for wrong/timeout.
  • Performance Levels: 20 points = Smart Performer • 30 points = Quiz Master • 50 points = Ultimate Legend.
  • Passing System: The second team gets one chance on a passed question. After that, the next question begins.
  • Turn System: A team that answers correctly keeps control for the next question. The turn switches only when the current team answers incorrectly or fails/time-outs.
  • Answer Reveal: The correct answer stays hidden while a question is passed — it's shown only once it's answered correctly, or once both teams have had their attempt.
